## Deck Composition

**Player Body Pile (10 cards):**
- 6x Skeleton
- 3x Zombie
- 1x Ghost

**Player Equipment Pile (15 cards):**
- 5x Rusty Axe
- 5x Shield
- 3x Helmet
- 2x Iron Sword

**Enemy Deck (20 cards):**
- 8x Squire
- 6x Knight
- 4x Barbarian
- 2x Thief

---

## Controls

| Action | Input |
|--------|-------|
| Draw from Body Pile | Click "Draw from BODY Pile" button |
| Draw from Equipment Pile | Click "Draw from EQUIPMENT Pile" button |
| Summon Unit | Drag body card to empty lane |
| Equip Unit | Drag equipment card onto unit |
| End Turn | Click "END TURN" button |

---

## Turn Phases

1. **DRAW** - Choose pile, draw 2 cards
2. **PLAY** - Summon + equip (unlimited actions)
3. **COMBAT** - Player attacks first, then enemies counter
4. **CLEANUP** - Enemy spawns, new turn

---

## Win/Loss Conditions

**WIN**: All enemies dead + enemy deck empty  
**LOSS**: Player HP reaches 0

---

## Player HP System

**Starting HP**: 20  
**Damage Sources**:
- Overflow damage (when enemy kills your unit, excess damage hits you)
- Direct attacks (when lane has no player unit to block)

**Example**:
- Enemy with 5 ATK attacks your 2 HP Skeleton
- Your Skeleton dies, 3 overflow damage → You take 3 damage!

---

## Tips

- **Player attacks first** - Use high ATK to kill before counter!
- **Manage HP carefully** - You only have 20 HP!
- **Block threats** - Always have units to prevent direct damage
- **Overkill matters** - Big enemies can overflow damage through weak units
- **Early Game**: Focus on bodies (need board presence)
- **Mid Game**: Balance bodies + equipment (quality vs quantity)
- **Late Game**: Equip survivors (scaling power)
- **Skeletons**: Best equipment targets (2 slots), can one-shot with +3 sword
- **Zombies**: Tank damage, equip defensively (prevent overflow)
- **Ghosts**: Glass cannon, equip weapons to kill enemies first
